* Purpose
Star KB is the shared, durable knowledge-base corpus used by StarIntel actors and services. It centralizes reusable Prolog facts, rules, modules, provenance, and query contracts so individual actors do not invent incompatible local schemas.

* Current phase
Research is approved for the initial dialect / KB direction. Deep design is the active gate. Implementation is not approved yet.

* Roadmap
1. Consolidate approved research into this repository.
2. Review and approve the initial Prolog KB/query-server design.
3. After explicit approval, implement the minimal queryable KB skeleton.
4. Grow reusable domain modules and ingestion/provenance tooling incrementally.
5. Integrate actors through the shared query contract rather than actor-specific schemas.
