* Research status
This note records the already-approved research direction that motivated =star-kb=. It is intentionally research, not implementation design.

* Problem
StarIntel needs a large, durable body of reusable facts and rules that can be consumed by many actors without duplicating knowledge models or embedding actor-specific schemas everywhere.

* Observed direction
- Prolog is the primary representation for reusable symbolic facts and rules.
- The corpus should live in a dedicated repository rather than inside individual actors.
- Consumers should normally access the corpus through the existing Prolog query-server boundary instead of loading arbitrary files directly.
- Knowledge should be partitioned into modules / domains so consumers can request bounded subsets instead of loading the whole corpus.
- Provenance must remain attached to sourced facts so imported observations can be distinguished from inference and manually curated knowledge.
- The structure must support long-term growth: many files, many domains, reusable rule packages, and independent evolution of producers and consumers.

* Candidate execution layers considered
** Prolog
Best fit for the canonical fact/rule corpus and symbolic reasoning layer. It provides direct representation of predicates, rules, constraints, and query semantics.

** Lisp wrapper
Useful as an integration/runtime layer around Prolog services and StarIntel actors, particularly where orchestration, service boundaries, or existing Common Lisp components are involved.

** Star language
A future candidate for higher-level authoring and orchestration as StarLang matures. Star KB must not depend on unfinished StarLang features for its initial corpus contract.

** Deep / neuro-symbolic learning
Useful as an enrichment or inference producer, not as a replacement for the canonical symbolic corpus. Learned outputs should enter through explicit provenance-bearing ingestion boundaries.

* Research conclusions
1. Maintain one shared, modular Prolog corpus in =star-kb=.
2. Treat the Prolog query server as the normal consumer boundary.
3. Separate canonical stored facts from derived/inferred results.
4. Require provenance for externally sourced or generated facts.
5. Make modules independently loadable and queryable.
6. Keep actor-specific transport/orchestration outside the core knowledge representation.
7. Design for StarLang and neuro-symbolic producers to integrate later without changing the meaning of existing predicates.

* Open questions for design
- Exact on-disk package/module layout.
- Predicate naming, namespaces, and compatibility rules.
- Provenance representation and source identity model.
- Query-server module-selection and result-envelope contract.
- Versioning and migration policy for predicates.
- Validation/linting required before new knowledge enters the corpus.
- How generated/inferred knowledge is quarantined or promoted.
- How actors discover available modules and predicates.
